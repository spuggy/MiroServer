Meticulous and thoughtful in nature, as an ISTJ (Inspector) in a leadership role you are likely to give clear and consistent with your customers, colleagues and juniors alike. Inspector leaders give well-defined, straightforward instructions and expect those around them to plan in an orderly and logical fashion. As an Analyser pivoting on Introverted Sensing (Si) the Inspector will gather information and rely on his or her own expertise and considerable knowledge-base to assess situations and possible outcomes before acting. Inspectors may be impatient with those who do not seem equally systematic in their approach.

The Inspector’s Auxiliary Function of Extroverted Thinking (Ti) will also see them keen to take charge when things are not proceeding as they would like, organising others and potentially micro managing work that they feel they could do better. Inspector leaders are likely to take their time about planning and decision-making and will feel the need to be able to explain their rationale and review decisions if necessary. Detached and fair-minded in their dealings with staff, colleagues and customers, they make excellent diplomats and problem solvers.

Inspectors may need to be careful not to become bogged down in the minutia of a situation however, possibly losing sight of broader objectives as a result. They might also need to remind themselves to be aware of other people’s emotional and personal responses to working conditions and objectives. What may seem like a perfectly reasonable desire for self-discipline to an Inspector in a leadership role may feel like a straightjacket to others. As an Inspector leader you will need to stay connected to your team and particularly the Feeling and iNtuitive types around you, in order to maintain optimism and good relations within the group and not to lose sight of the bigger picture.

Your Leadership Potential:

  • Finding Practical solutions
  • Making clear audited decisions
  • Staying calm in a crisis
  • Paying attention to detail
  • Delivering high quality outcomes
  • Diplomatic, open and honest communication

Your Possible struggles:

  • Communicating abstract or ambiguous ideas
  • Awareness of emotions in others
  • Dealing with Feeling or iNtuitive types over prolonged periods
  • Operating outside of predefined structures

Your Leadership Temperament is: Guardian (SJ)

The Guardian Inspector wants accuracy and conscientiousness from his or her team and has little tolerance for errors.

Flash points for Guardian leaders are:

  • Those who don't take their work seriously
  • Those who break the company rules
  • Those who do not respect their position and follow order
